Which companies are growing, becoming more profitable and performing consistently?

Management needs a like-for-like view of how a group of listed companies have grown, how profitable that growth has been and whose financial position is improving or weakening. Every figure on this page is calculated live from the data and responds to the filters above.

    How to use this dashboard

    1. Start on Overview. The KPI cards, key findings and recommended actions summarise the current view.
    2. Filter. Choose years, categories or companies in the bar at the top. Every chart, table, KPI, report and export follows the filters. Active filters appear as chips; click a chip to remove it, or use Reset filters.
    3. Investigate. Hover any chart for exact values. Click a company in a chart to open its profile (Ratios & market tab). Use ⤢ to view a chart full screen.
    4. Compare fairly. In the trend charts, switch to "Index" to compare companies of very different size, and highlight up to three companies.
    5. Add data. On Add data, upload a file, read the check, then apply it. Charts and KPIs update straight away. Undo or reset at any time.
    6. Take it away. Generate Analysis Report creates a PDF of the current or full analysis; Download Analysis Data exports the records being analysed (CSV or Excel).

    Data and its limits

    • Source: , a public demonstration dataset of 12 listed companies (161 company-years, 2009-2023) supplied for this project. Company names are added from the tickers for readability.
    • Units: revenue, profit and cash flows in USD millions; market capitalisation in USD billions; ratios in %.
    • Uneven coverage: most companies report to 2022; Microsoft and NVIDIA to 2023; Sears ends in 2018; PayPal starts in 2014. Year-on-year KPIs compare only companies reporting in both years.
    • Not meaningful values: banks and insurers have no gross margin or current ratio in this data; ROE and debt/equity are not shown where equity is negative.
    • Fiscal years end in different months; "year" is the fiscal year as reported.
    • Associations only: correlations across about a dozen companies are indicative and do not show cause and effect.
    • Where your data is kept: uploads stay in this browser's IndexedDB. Clear them with "Reset to the original dataset" or by clearing site data in the browser.
